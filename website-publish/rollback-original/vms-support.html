Before contacting support

A few details help us solve the issue faster.

For VMS / camera issues

  • Customer or site name.
  • Which camera or cameras are affected.
  • Whether live view, recording, playback or events are affected.
  • Approximate time the problem started.
  • Any error message shown on screen.
  • Whether the Edge appliance is powered and connected to the network.

For AAC Face Access issues

  • Site and door/access-point name.
  • Whether the issue is recognition, access decision, provider API or relay operation.
  • Approximate time of the failed event.
  • Whether the AI Access Edge is online.
  • Do not email passwords, API secrets or private access codes.
Camera credentials: AnyAiCam support does not bypass manufacturer security. If a camera or recorder password is unknown, use the approved manufacturer recovery/reset process. Physical reset, cabling or onsite work may require the customer or a local technician.
