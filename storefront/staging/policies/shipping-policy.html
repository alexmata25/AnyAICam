Shipping Policy

Draft — last updated for internal review.

Preparation and shipping timeframe

Please allow up to 14 days for preparation and shipment.

AnyAiCam appliances are not shipped directly off a shelf. Before your order ships, AnyAiCam sources the hardware, installs and configures the AnyAiCam VMS software on it, and tests the unit to confirm it is working correctly. This preparation step is what the 14-day window covers.

Why timing can vary

Shipment timing can vary based on hardware availability. While we aim to prepare and ship every order within the stated window, availability of components or finished units can occasionally extend that timeframe. If your order is expected to take meaningfully longer than 14 days, we will do our best to let you know.

Tracking

Once your order ships, we will provide tracking information so you can follow its progress.

Delivery time

Delivery time after shipment is determined by the shipping carrier, not by AnyAiCam. We do not promise an exact delivery date unless the carrier's own tracking information provides one, and even then that estimate comes from the carrier, not from us.

After delivery

When your appliance arrives, sign in to your AnyAiCam account at app.anyaicam.com to complete setup and connect your cameras.
