Hardware Return & Refund Policy

Draft — applies to physical AnyAiCam hardware appliances and accessories (e.g. Ryzen appliances, the Numato relay module). Does not apply to Local or Hybrid camera-slot subscriptions or analytics subscriptions — see the separate Cancellation Policy for those.

Order not yet shipped

If your hardware order has not yet shipped, you may request a cancellation. Any refund for a cancelled unshipped order is subject to this policy, including the restocking fee described below where applicable.

Order already shipped

Once an order has shipped, it cannot simply be cancelled as though it never happened. Instead, it must go through our return process: a return request, return authorization, shipping the equipment back to us, and inspection, before any refund is issued.

Why configured hardware is different

Because AnyAiCam installs, configures, and tests VMS software on your specific appliance before it ships, a returned unit requires additional handling compared to an unopened, unconfigured product. For this reason, an approved return may be subject to a Restocking Fee, where legally permitted.

Flag for legal review: "configured/customized hardware" framing and whether this is sufficient basis for a restocking fee in every jurisdiction where AnyAiCam sells.

Restocking Fee

An eligible, approved hardware return is subject to a 15% Restocking Fee. The Restocking Fee helps cover legitimate costs associated with receiving returned hardware, inspection, testing, reconfiguration, repackaging, inventory handling, and payment-processing/administrative costs, where legally permitted.

The Restocking Fee is the single clearly disclosed deduction from a hardware refund. AnyAiCam does not separately charge a "processing fee," "bank fee," or "Stripe fee" on a customer's return unless a future, separately approved and legally reviewed policy explicitly says otherwise.

Exception — defective or damaged hardware: no Restocking Fee applies when the hardware is defective due to AnyAiCam or arrives damaged, unless a final, legally reviewed policy later says otherwise.

Flag for legal review: the 15% Restocking Fee figure is an approved BUSINESS value, not yet legally reviewed. Do not publish this policy live until it has been reviewed and separately approved for publication.

Return window

Customers have 30 days from delivery (or from shipment if the delivery date is unknown) to request an eligible hardware return.

Flag for legal review: the 30-day return window is an approved BUSINESS value, not yet legally reviewed.

Refund calculation (example)

An approved refund is calculated as the original purchase amount, less the applicable Restocking Fee:

Original eligible hardware amount: $1,249.99
15% Restocking Fee: $187.50
Final refund amount (before any other approved policy adjustment): $1,062.49

If the return qualifies for the defective/damaged exception above, no Restocking Fee is deducted and the final refund amount is the full original amount.

AnyAiCam does not automatically deduct shipping, taxes, or payment-processing costs from a refund beyond the disclosed Restocking Fee, unless a future policy explicitly and separately permits it.

Flag for legal review: non-refundable shipping charges (if any are ever introduced) and payment-processing deductions — both intentionally absent from this draft pending decision and review.

Damaged or missing items

Returned equipment is inspected before a refund is approved. If accessories are missing or the equipment shows damage beyond normal use (and the defective/damaged-on-arrival exception above does not apply), the approved refund amount may be adjusted accordingly.

Flag for legal review: exact standards for what counts as damage/missing items, and how a refund adjustment for it is calculated and disclosed, beyond the defective/damaged-on-arrival exception already described above.

Return process

  1. Contact AnyAiCam support to request a return.
  2. If approved, you will receive a return authorization with instructions and a reference number.
  3. Ship the equipment back, including all accessories that were originally included.
  4. Once received, the equipment is inspected.
  5. A final refund amount is determined and, once approved, processed.
