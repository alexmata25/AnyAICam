Cancellation Policy

Draft. AnyAiCam hardware purchases and Local/Hybrid camera-slot subscriptions are cancelled under different rules — each is covered separately below.

Hardware appliance orders

An unshipped hardware order (Ryzen appliance or Numato relay module) may be cancelled on request; any applicable refund follows our Hardware Return & Refund Policy, including the disclosed Restocking Fee where applicable.

A hardware order that has already shipped cannot be cancelled outright — it must instead go through the return process described in the Hardware Return & Refund Policy.

Local / Hybrid camera-slot subscriptions

Local and Hybrid camera-slot subscriptions are recurring software/service products, not physical hardware, and are cancelled under their own rules — never treated as a "hardware return."

  • Cancelling stops future renewal according to the approved subscription cancellation policy.
  • Your AnyAiCam account is preserved.
  • Any connected appliance and its Cloud ID are preserved.
  • Your camera-slot entitlement becomes 0 at the appropriate cancellation effective time.
Flag for legal review: the exact cancellation-effective timing (e.g. immediate vs. end of current billing period) and any prorated-refund treatment for a subscription cancelled mid-cycle — not yet finalized.

Analytics subscriptions

Analytics add-on subscriptions follow the same recurring-service cancellation treatment as Local/Hybrid subscriptions, not the hardware return process.
