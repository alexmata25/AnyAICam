AnyAiCam VMS

Your cameras. Your edge appliance. One video system.

AnyAiCam VMS is designed to operate compatible security cameras from an AnyAiCam Edge appliance. The appliance handles the local camera connection, recording and core VMS services while optional cloud services extend remote access, protected storage and analytics.

What it does

A complete operating layer for compatible cameras

The VMS is the software that turns a group of compatible cameras into one managed security system instead of a collection of separate devices.

◉

Live View

Open authorized cameras from the AnyAiCam interface and see the current scene without logging into every camera separately.

▶

Recording & Playback

Record video on the edge appliance and review footage by camera and time. Hybrid cloud options can add off-site protection.

⚡

Events

Surface motion and AI-generated events with event media so customers can move from an alert to the related video quickly.

AI

Modular Analytics

Enable licensed analytics per camera or camera slot rather than forcing every camera into the same feature package.

☁

Hybrid Cloud

Keep the appliance as the operating center while using cloud services for customer access, selected media, entitlements and optional retention.

⌁

Remote Management

Associate customers, sites, appliances and camera entitlements so the system can be supported without rebuilding it for every new installation.

Local-first architecture

The edge appliance stays in the middle of the design

The website sells and provisions the system. The edge appliance performs the local camera work. That separation is important: a public website should not try to scan a customer's private LAN directly.

What customers buy

Camera Slots & Features
