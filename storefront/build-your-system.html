Choose local / hybrid storage behavior

The Edge appliance remains the local operating center. Cloud retention is an optional extension.

V1 storage baseline: for 4MP / 15 FPS / H.265 motion recording at about 25% activity, plan roughly 1 / 2 / 4 TB for 8 cameras at 7 / 14 / 30 days, and 2 / 4 / 8 TB for 16 cameras. Actual retention varies; continuous recording and 8MP/4K require substantially more capacity.
Production pricing: final Edge, storage and feature prices will map to approved products later. This public planner does not create a charge.

These details identify the customer and site the future paid-order provisioning manifest would belong to. This public planner does not charge a card.

What happens after verified payment in the production design
  1. Stripe verifies payment server-to-server.
  2. The backend creates a canonical provisioning request with camera slots and purchased feature quantities.
  3. AnyAiCam creates/links the customer, site, appliance reservation and entitlements.
  4. The appliance is fulfilled and associated with that provisioning record.
  5. After delivery, the customer connects it, signs in/activates, discovers cameras, enters camera credentials locally and assigns purchased feature slots.
Planning only: no physical Edge appliance, AWS resources, access-control integration or production subscription is created from this page.
