FOR ANYAICAM VMS

What we check before you buy.

A quick look at your cameras, network and storage tells us what can stay and what AnyAiCam setup fits your site. It is free and it is not a subscription.

  • Your camerasAnyAiCam works with compatible IP (network) cameras that share a standard video stream, usually through ONVIF or RTSP. If your cameras connect through an NVR, we check whether it can share their streams. Many brands work; we confirm your models before you buy.
  • ResolutionMost systems use 2MP to 8MP cameras. Higher resolution and frame rate look sharper, but need more storage and processing.
  • Network and powerCameras should be on the same local network as your AnyAiCam appliance or PC. Wired Ethernet, often with a PoE switch that powers the cameras, is the most reliable choice.
  • Camera accessYou will usually need each camera’s administrator username and password once during setup. We never reset or bypass passwords.
  • Processing capacityHow many cameras one appliance or PC runs well depends on the hardware, camera resolution, stream settings and the analytics you use. Your plan can license up to 64 cameras; we help you size the hardware for your actual workload.
  • StorageRecording storage depends on the number of cameras, their bitrate, motion or continuous recording, and how many days you keep. Build Your System shows a rough guide.
  • Heavier analyticsLicense Plate Recognition, People Counting and PPE detection need more processing than recording and basic motion alerts, so they can lower how many cameras one appliance handles.

HOW THE ASSESSMENT WORKS

A clear answer before you commit to new equipment.

Many camera systems are still useful, but customers are often told to replace everything before anyone carefully reviews what they own. ANY AI CAM takes a different approach.

  1. IdentifyTell us the camera, NVR, or DVR brand and model—or ask for help locating it.
  2. EvaluateWe determine whether the system is IP, analog or ONVIF-capable, and whether AnyAiCam VMS or Videoloft cloud fits it.
  3. RecommendWe explain what can stay, what may need replacing, and whether local installation work is required.
  4. ConnectFor supported systems, we help you set up AnyAiCam VMS or activate Videoloft cloud viewing and recording.
