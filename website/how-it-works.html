Customer Steps

Cloud activation, step by step.

These are the general steps after you request cloud activation. Final setup depends on your selected path, camera system, recorder, and network.

Before purchasing: confirm camera access

Videoloft normally needs the administrator username and password for the camera or recorder during the one-time connection process. If those credentials are unavailable, the original installer, a technician, or a manufacturer-approved recovery process may be needed first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I try the service without buying an adapter?

Yes. The Windows Software Bridge is free, so compatible customers can use it with the 30-day cloud trial without purchasing adapter hardware. The computer must stay powered on for cameras to remain connected.

What is a Cloud ID?

A Cloud ID is the code used by the CCTV Connect app to connect your site or recorder to cloud service.

What camera brands are supported?

Many compatible IP camera and recorder systems are commonly used, including Axis, Dahua, Lorex, LTS, Hikvision, Reolink, TVT, Uniview, Vivotek, Hanwha Vision, Digital Watchdog, ONVIF systems, and setups using a cloud adapter.

What happens after I submit my request?

ANY AI CAM reviews your submitted details and prepares the cloud account setup. If adapter hardware is needed, the adapter order and cloud plan request can move forward together.

How do payments work?

Hardware purchases are paid securely at checkout. Cloud storage is billed after activation, and new cloud customers receive a 30-day free trial before recurring cloud billing begins.

How do I avoid fake activation emails?

Do not send passwords or card details by email. Real website requests include a request ID, and ANY AI CAM will review details before sending next steps.

Security Camera Cloud Storage Questions

Can I keep my existing cameras and still use cloud storage?

Often, yes. Compatible IP cameras and NVR systems can add cloud recording without replacing the entire system. Check your camera compatibility before ordering.

Do I need to replace my NVR or DVR?

Not always. Many systems can connect through a cloud adapter or a supported Windows software bridge.

How much video history can I keep?

Choose from 7-day, 14-day or 30-day retention options on our cloud storage pricing page.

Can I buy a camera without cloud service?

Yes. Visit our camera shop for the current camera offering and optional cloud-service choices.
