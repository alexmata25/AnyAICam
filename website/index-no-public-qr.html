Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Cloud ID?

A Cloud ID is the code used by the CCTV Connect app to connect your site or recorder to cloud service.

What camera brands are supported?

Many compatible IP camera and recorder systems are commonly used, including Axis, Dahua, Lorex, LTS, Hikvision, Reolink, TVT, Uniview, Vivotek, Hanwha Vision, Digital Watchdog, ONVIF systems, and setups using a cloud adapter.

What happens after I submit my request?

ANY AI CAM reviews your submitted details and prepares the cloud account setup. If adapter hardware is needed, the adapter order and cloud plan request can move forward together.

How do payments work?

Cloud storage is billed monthly by Videoloft directly to the customer after the 30-day free trial. You pay only when service is activated - no upfront charges.

How do I avoid fake activation emails?

Do not send passwords or card details by email. Real website requests include a request ID, and ANY AI CAM will review details before sending next steps.
