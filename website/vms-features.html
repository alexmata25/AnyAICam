Entitlements follow the camera slot

A camera slot represents purchased capacity and feature rights. That makes it possible to replace a failed camera without permanently tying the subscription to an IP address.

Example slotCameraLicenseCloud
Slot 01Front EntranceAnalytics14-day hybrid
Slot 02LobbyAnalytics14-day hybrid
Slot 03StorageBasicLocal only
Why this matters: if the Front Entrance camera is replaced, the new device can be assigned to Slot 01 and inherit the purchased rights after authorization and commissioning.
