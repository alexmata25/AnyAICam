Build the system before the box arrives.

Choose your AnyAiCam system, number of cameras and monthly plan. See your one-time and monthly costs, then continue to checkout.

How many cameras will this site use?

Your monthly plan is priced per licensed camera, from 1 to 64 cameras per account.

Licenses are not tied to one device: if a camera fails, its replacement takes over that camera license after authorization.

Choose the AnyAiCam appliance

For homes, choose the AnyAiCam Residential VMS — Ryzen 5. For VMS-only business systems choose the AnyAiCam Starter (MINISFORUM AI X1-255 · Ryzen 7 255) or the AnyAiCam Professional (MINISFORUM AI X1 · Ryzen AI 9 HX 470). The AnyAiCam Enterprise appliance is for commercial AAC Face Access with larger face databases.

Facial recognition runs on the AnyAiCam Residential VMS — Ryzen 5 for one front-door camera and up to 5 household members, or on the AnyAiCam Enterprise appliance for commercial Face Access, more face cameras and larger face databases. Starter, Professional and your own PC are unavailable while Face Access is above 0.
Important hardware boundary

The Numato three-channel relay is the door-control hardware for AnyAiCam Face Access and can also trigger a door or gate on its own. It is not an appliance, and VMS-only systems do not need it. Exact production Edge models and capacities will be published only after AnyAiCam validation.

Choose your monthly plan

One plan covers every licensed camera on your account. You pay per camera, per month.

Longer local recording (14 or 30 days) does not change the plan price; it needs enough recording storage. For 4MP / 15 FPS / H.265 motion recording at about 25% activity, plan roughly 1 / 2 / 4 TB for 8 cameras at 7 / 14 / 30 days, and 2 / 4 / 8 TB for 16 cameras. Continuous recording and 8MP/4K need substantially more.
Coming soon: continuous cloud recording and cloud overflow, as separate paid options.

Choose how many cameras get each feature

Smart Motion and person/vehicle detection are included in AI Local and Hybrid. AnyAiCam confirms any other analytics with you.

Feature quantities are purchased independently. For example, a 10-camera site can buy Smart Motion for 5 cameras and basic VMS for the other 5.

Integrated Face Access connection

Coming soon: integrations with enterprise access-control platforms such as Brivo or Avigilon. None is available yet.

Numato relay door control (for AAC Face Access or on its own)
Numato 3-channel relay — $149.99

Use for AAC Face Access door control or for local door/gate triggering on its own. This is not the VMS/AI Edge appliance and is separate from AAC Face Access platform integrations.

Each module ($149.99) provides three Ethernet-controlled SPDT relay outputs. Final installations must use appropriate listed access-control/lock hardware and preserve required egress/life-safety behavior.

Commercial AAC Face Access on the AnyAiCam Enterprise appliance needs a door-control choice (on the AnyAiCam Residential VMS — Ryzen 5, door control is optional): AnyAiCam Face Access (available now) or a third-party platform integration (coming soon).
