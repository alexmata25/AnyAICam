AnyAiCam VMS

Your cameras. Your AnyAiCam appliance. One video system.

AnyAiCam VMS is designed to operate compatible security cameras from an AnyAiCam appliance. The appliance handles the local camera connection, recording and core VMS services while optional cloud services extend remote access, protected storage and analytics.

What it does

A complete operating layer for compatible cameras

The VMS is the software that turns a group of compatible cameras into one managed security system instead of a collection of separate devices.

◉

Live View

Open authorized cameras from the AnyAiCam interface and see the current scene without logging into every camera separately.

▶

Recording & Playback

Record video on the AnyAiCam appliance and review footage by camera and time. Hybrid cloud options can add off-site protection.

⚡

Events

Surface motion and AI-generated events with event media so customers can move from an alert to the related video quickly.

AI

Modular Analytics

Enable licensed analytics per camera or camera slot rather than forcing every camera into the same feature package.

☁

Hybrid Cloud

Keep the appliance as the operating center while using cloud services for customer access, selected media, entitlements and optional retention.

⌁

Remote Management

Associate customers, sites, appliances and camera entitlements so the system can be supported without rebuilding it for every new installation.

In the AnyAiCam app

Watch live, then find exactly what happened

Open any camera live in full size, or go back through the day on a timeline that marks every person, vehicle and motion event.

Local-first architecture

The AnyAiCam appliance stays in the middle of the design

The website sells and provisions the system. The AnyAiCam appliance performs the local camera work. That separation is important: a public website should not try to scan a customer's private LAN directly.

What customers buy

Hardware plus the services they actually need

Required

AnyAiCam Appliance

The always-on local system that connects to cameras, runs VMS services and stores local video according to the selected system design.

Per-camera

Camera Slots & Features

Purchased camera capacity is assigned as logical slots. Features can be licensed per slot, making camera replacement easier without repurchasing the whole system.

Optional

Cloud Retention

Select hybrid/off-site storage and a retention target when the site requires cloud-protected video in addition to local recording.

Premium / future

AAC Facial Recognition + Door Access

On the AnyAiCam Enterprise appliance, an authorized face match passes through an access policy and requests a door unlock. Access-control platform integrations are coming soon. See AAC Face Access →
