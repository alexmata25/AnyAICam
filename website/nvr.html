How the Adapter Connects

Plug into your existing network, not your system.

Your Existing Cameras

Your compatible IP cameras stay connected to your existing network or NVR setup. Analog/coax DVR cameras are not directly compatible without a manual review or upgrade path.

  • Keep your existing camera wiring and positions
  • All existing recording schedules and motion settings remain
  • Works with compatible IP/network camera systems

Your Existing NVR / DVR

Your recorder stays as your primary recording device. The Videoloft Cloud Adapter connects to your network alongside it - not in place of it.

  • Local recording continues without interruption
  • No firmware changes needed on your recorder
  • Adapter connects to your network switch, not your recorder directly

The Videoloft Cloud Adapter

A small hardware device that connects to your network switch or router. It discovers your recorder on the network and securely transmits video to the ANY AI CAM cloud.

  • Plug into any available Ethernet port on your network switch
  • Automatically discovers compatible recorders on your network
  • No computer needed - runs 24/7 on its own
  • View adapter models and pricing

Visual Overview

How everything connects together.

  1. Cameras - Your existing cameras connect to your NVR or DVR as they always have.
  2. NVR / DVR - Your recorder continues local recording and connects to your network switch.
  3. Videoloft Cloud Adapter - Plugs into the same network switch, discovers your recorder, and sends video to the cloud.
  4. Router - The adapter uses your existing internet connection to reach the ANY AI CAM cloud.
  5. Videoloft Cloud - Your video is encrypted, stored offsite by Videoloft, and accessible from anywhere.

Your existing system never changes. The Videoloft Cloud Adapter connects to your network and bridges your recorder to the cloud.

Requirements

What you need to add the cloud adapter.

Network Requirements

  • Broadband internet connection (minimum 5 Mbps upload recommended)
  • Network switch or router with an available Ethernet port for the adapter
  • Your NVR and adapter must be on the same local network

NVR Requirements

  • ONVIF-compatible NVR or DVR (most major brands supported)
  • Network-connected recorder with accessible web interface
  • Cameras already configured and recording on the NVR

ANY AI CAM reviews your specific NVR model and network setup during activation. If your recorder needs firmware updates or network changes, we guide you through the steps.
