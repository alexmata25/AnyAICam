V1 recording-storage guide

Size recording storage separately from the appliance system SSD.

The 1 TB SSD in the sourced MINISFORUM configurations is primarily system storage for the OS, VMS, containers, logs, updates and AI software. AnyAiCam recording capacity should be selected separately.

Planning baseline: H.265, 4MP, 15 FPS, motion/event recording at approximately 25% activity. Actual retention varies with camera bitrate, scene complexity, night noise, frame rate and motion level.

AnyAiCam Professional

MINISFORUM AI X1 · Ryzen AI 9 HX 470 · $1,749.99

Practical V1 approach: 1 TB system SSD plus 4 TB recording storage for typical 8–16-camera motion/event designs, with external/NAS capacity for larger or longer-retention systems.

Capacity policy: do not plan to fill the recording volume completely. AnyAiCam sizing should preserve free space for retention rotation, indexes, thumbnails, temporary clips and recovery. Continuous recording and 8MP/4K cameras require substantially more storage.

VMS, integrated Face Access and standalone access hardware

Simple rule: VMS-only = AnyAiCam Starter (MINISFORUM AI X1-255 · Ryzen 7 255) or AnyAiCam Professional (MINISFORUM AI X1 · Ryzen AI 9 HX 470). Integrated AAC Face Access with a supported provider API = AnyAiCam Enterprise appliance. Standalone door/gate control = Numato relay path. The relay is not a required accessory for the VMS or AI Edge appliances.
