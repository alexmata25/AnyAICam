The AAC features build on it: an assistant you can simply ask, a visitor call when someone comes to your door, and armed security with intrusion alarms. Everything runs on the same appliance and shows up in the same AnyAiCam app.

Two kinds of alerts

Everyday activity and real alarms never look the same

Normal camera activity, such as a person walking up the driveway or a car in the street, arrives as a calm alert in Smart Alerts. An INTRUSION ALARM from Secure Edge is urgent, red and clearly labeled, so you always know which one needs you now.

Everyday alert

INTRUSION ALARM
