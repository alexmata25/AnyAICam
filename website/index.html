ANYAICAM VMS EDGE APPLIANCES

Choose the Edge class that matches the job.

VMS-only customers should not pay for the dedicated facial-recognition appliance. Use the AnyAiCam Starter (MINISFORUM AI X1-255 · Ryzen 7 255) as the standard VMS appliance, choose the AnyAiCam Professional (MINISFORUM AI X1 · Ryzen AI 9 HX 470) when the site needs more compute headroom, and use the separate AnyAiCam Enterprise appliance only when AAC Face Access is part of the system.

AnyAiCam Starter

MINISFORUM AI X1-255 · Ryzen 7 255 · $1,249.99

Standard AnyAiCam VMS Edge appliance for local recording, live view, playback and approved analytics. The sourced configuration shown is 32 GB DDR5 with a 1 TB system SSD; recording storage is sized separately.

AnyAiCam Professional

MINISFORUM AI X1 · Ryzen AI 9 HX 470 · $1,749.99

Higher-performance AnyAiCam VMS Edge appliance for heavier VMS and analytics workloads or planned expansion. The sourced configuration shown is 32 GB DDR5 with a 1 TB system SSD; recording storage is sized separately.

AnyAiCam Enterprise — Face Access

MINISFORUM AI X1 Pro-470 · $2,249.99

Separate local AI appliance for AAC facial recognition and access-policy workflows. Liveness checks and access-control platform integrations are coming soon. It is not the standard VMS recording appliance.

AAC FACE ACCESS + STANDALONE ACCESS

AAC Face Access with Numato relay door control.

AAC Face Access uses the AnyAiCam Enterprise appliance, with the Numato three-channel relay for door control; access-control platform integrations are coming soon. The relay can also be used on its own for local door/gate trigger control, and VMS-only systems on the AnyAiCam Starter (MINISFORUM AI X1-255 · Ryzen 7 255) or AnyAiCam Professional (MINISFORUM AI X1 · Ryzen AI 9 HX 470) do not need it. The video below is a third-party industry example of a person using facial recognition at an intercom.

AnyAiCam Enterprise — Face Access

MINISFORUM AI X1 Pro-470 · $2,249.99

Higher-performance local compute for customers who purchase AAC Face Access (access-control platform integrations coming soon). Standard VMS-only systems should use the AnyAiCam Starter (MINISFORUM AI X1-255 · Ryzen 7 255) or AnyAiCam Professional (MINISFORUM AI X1 · Ryzen AI 9 HX 470).
